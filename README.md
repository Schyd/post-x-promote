# Post-X Promote: Make Your Own Bounty

One X post quoting make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post, with a photo of its own attached. The post is scored by its replies + reposts + quotes at the deadline — views and likes do not count — and the top 50 entries share $250.

The post handed in here is https://x.com/KangBub49695669/status/2125515798588633446 — it quotes make.fun's post, carries a photo made for it, and its text carries a make.fun link, the @makefunai tag, and a make.fun play code.
